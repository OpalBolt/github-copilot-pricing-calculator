# Take OpenCode Zen prices from models.dev

models.dev is a third-party aggregate, which the first-party price policy (ADR 0002) normally excludes. OpenCode Zen publishes no priced catalog of its own, and models.dev is OpenCode's own resource, so it is treated as first-party-adjacent for Zen only. The site names models.dev as Zen's price source in its price note. This exception applies to Zen and to no other seller.
